## What shipped

- `packages/tallynet/src/trace.ts`: one additive variant on the `TraceEvent` union —
  `{ type: 'record-pulled'; transactionCode: string; from: string; ok: boolean }`, mirroring `record-pushed`.
  `from` is the serving peer's public member-address key; `ok` is whether a reply came back. No
  `TRACE_FORMAT_VERSION` bump (a new consensus event needs none). The existing disclosure-invariant comment
  already covers `to`/`from` as public addresses — no comment change needed.
- `packages/tallynet/src/consensus/participant.ts`: `fetchOutcome` emits one `record-pulled` per reachable
  peer right after that peer's `fetchRecord` resolves, via a new `emitPulled` helper beside `emitPushed`.
  Pure instrumentation through the `safeTraceSink`-wrapped sink — no control-flow change.
- Tests: a `participant.test.ts` unit asserting per-peer `ok:true`/`ok:false`, and an extension to the
  existing "interior refuser cuts the far member off" e2e asserting F's three reachable peers each get a
  `record-pulled` (`ok:true` from the two contact points, `ok:false` from the refusing link-neighbour).
