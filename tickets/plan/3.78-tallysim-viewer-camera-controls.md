Today the viewer (sigma.js, 2D) pans by dragging and zooms by wheel or pinch, but nothing on screen says so, and there are no buttons. A developer tried it and didn't find the controls.

## Scope

- An on-screen control cluster: zoom in, zoom out, fit to graph, reset, rotate left/right. Use sigma's camera (`getCamera().animate`, `animatedZoom`/`animatedUnzoom`/`animatedReset`, camera `angle`).
- Keyboard shortcuts: `+`/`-` zoom, `0` fit/reset, arrow keys pan, `[`/`]` rotate. Only active while the graph has focus, never while typing in search or other inputs.
- A brief, dismissible hint naming the gestures (drag to pan, wheel/pinch to zoom).
- Labels and hover keep working at any rotation.
- Keep the headless core untouched; this is render/app code. Document the controls in `packages/tallysim-viewer/docs/design.md`.

Larger ideas for big graphs are in backlog: `feat-tallysim-viewer-3d` and `feat-tallysim-viewer-fisheye-lens`.
