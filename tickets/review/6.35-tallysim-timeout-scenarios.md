### The three scenarios (each a hand-built `line` network, read through `analyzeLift`)

1. **Late voter → voids by its own deadline.** `line(2)` `0—1`, sole voter node 0. Discovery runs on a
   **fast** sim (empty conditions) to mint a real `QueryOutcome`; the lift is then driven on a **second** sim
   over the *same* network with uniform `50_000 ms` latency and a short `ttlMs 80_000`, so the completing
   promise reaches the voter only post-expiry → `castCommit` votes `NC` → `void`. Asserts `outcome === 'void'`,
   `voidReason === 'voted-no-commit'` (a deadline void is a voter-vote `NC`, never `expired` — see memory
   `tallynet-expired-reason-unemitted`), balances unchanged, `timeToSettleMs` defined, `deliveryErrors === []`,
   `pendingTimers === 0`.
2. **Dropped commit-push → recovers by the loop.** `line(5)` `0—1—2—3—4`, node 2 a non-relayer, originator
   publishes a contact point. Reuses the existing record-availability construction but **collects trace
   events** and adds the assertion the ticket asked for: `analyzeLift(...).recovered === 1` (the protocol's own
   recovery loop ran, not a manual pull), plus `cooperatingCommitted === cooperatingMembers === 4` (completion
   1.0) and every member commits.
3. **Needed party never returns → abandons, stays `pending`.** `line(2)` `0—1`, lift driven on a sim whose
   sole link **drops** (`dropFraction: 1` picks the one link deterministically — no seed to re-derive), small
   `recoveryGiveupMs 60_000`. The push to the payee never lands, the promise phase never completes, the loop
   abandons. Asserts `outcome === 'pending'`, `voidReason === 'stalled'`, a `recovery-abandoned` event present,
   `permanentlyPending === true`, balances unchanged, `pendingTimers === 0`.

### Metrics + determinism

- A metrics test aggregates all three via `summarizeConsensusMetrics` and asserts: `timeToSettleMs.count === 2`

## Use cases to validate / where to push (your tests are a floor)

- **Scenario 1 uses `line(2)`, NOT the `line(3)` the ticket's resume-note suggested.** I verified by running
  that `line(3)` (voter = originator, two slow hops) lands **`pending`**, not `void`: the completing promise
  sits two-plus hops from the voter and the chained post-expiry recovery pulls do not reconverge it to the
  voter within the run. One slow hop (`line(2)`) is the clean deadline-void mirror and is documented at the
  test site. **Worth a reviewer's scrutiny:** is single-hop too degenerate a "late voter" story, or is it the
  honest minimal construction? A multi-voter deadline void (e.g. a separate instant-reached voter + a slow
  participant, à la tallynet :1249) would need a per-link latency the sim's uniform `ConditionsSpec` can't
  express — flag if you think that coverage gap matters.
- **Scenario 1's timing is empirically tuned** (`SLOW_LATENCY_MS`/`DEADLINE_TTL_MS`, invariant
  `L < ttl < 2L`). Parked as a `NOTE:` at the constants. Not seed-fragile (uniform constant latency), but
  schedule-fragile: a tallynet change to the deadline/recovery grace could flip it to commit or pending. The
  determinism tests would catch a *value* drift; they would not catch a *tallynet-schedule* change that keeps
  it deterministic but wrong-outcome — so if you touch tallynet's recovery timing, re-run this file.
- **Scenario 2 leans on the existing `voter-harness.test.ts` record-availability case** for the construction;
  I only added the trace-collection + `recovered`/`pullMessages` assertions. If you distrust `recovered` as a
  proxy for "the loop healed it", the per-member `commit` checks are the ground truth alongside it.
- **The two-sim trick (fast discovery, slow lift, one network)** is load-bearing for scenarios 1 and 3. It is
  sound because `reconstructRoute`/`buildLift` recompute nonces from `outcome.sessionCode` + the network's
  deterministic linkIds (sim-independent). Confirm you agree the fast-sim outcome drives the slow-sim lift
  byte-identically — if not, that invalidates both constructions.
