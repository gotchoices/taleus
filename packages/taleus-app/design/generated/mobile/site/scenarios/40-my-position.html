Scenario: My position

Source: 40-my-position.md

How a party is doing overall — and the three things the story is emphatic about.

Step 1: Owed and owing, per unit, where they are exact

Two quantities, not their difference. Broken out per unit, because that is where the figures are real. Zero takes no colour: 0 in red would be a warning about nothing.

Step 2: The estimate, and what it leaves out

One figure in the party's chosen unit, marked so it cannot be mistaken for a signed balance — a leading ≈ and the secondary colour, not just a caption. It names what it could not account for, and it shows owed and owing separately too: the rule above is not suspended because the figures are approximate. Where a rate runs both ways it takes the less favourable direction.

Step 3: What could be spent, kept apart from what is held

Credit available is never mixed into holdings. Spending power appears separately, in two parts: value others hold for this party, and credit others extended them.

Step 4: A party with nothing yet

Told what this will show, rather than shown empty totals.

Not shown

Tracing the estimate to what was converted at what rate; position over time; concentration by counterparty; settled value distinguished from movement not yet complete (paths C to E). ExchangeRates is unsliced, so the rates behind the estimate cannot be opened.
