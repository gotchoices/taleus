# Scenario: What needs me, and how I am doing

Source: [23-what-needs-my-attention.md](../../../stories/mobile/23-what-needs-my-attention.md),
[40-my-position.md](../../../stories/mobile/40-my-position.md)

## Step 1: Everything waiting, across every tally

Each item says who it involves, what it is, what it would cost, and how long it has waited. Items
waiting on somebody else appear plainly marked and never as a demand. Automated settling never
appears at all — it was authorized in advance and needs nothing.

![Attention](../images/attention-happy.png)

## Step 2: Nothing waiting

A good state, and it reads like one rather than like a failure to load.

![Nothing waiting](../images/attention-empty.png)

## Step 3: How the party is doing

Owed and owing as two quantities, per unit, where they are exact. The cross-unit figure below them is
an estimate and looks like one — a leading `≈`, the secondary colour — and it names what it left out
for want of a rate. Credit available is never mixed into what the party holds.

![Position](../images/position-happy.png)

## Step 4: Nothing yet

Told what this will show, rather than shown zeros.

![Nothing yet](../images/position-empty.png)

## Not shown

Setting an item aside, deadlines, and the record of past items (story 23 paths E and F); position
over time and concentration by counterparty (story 40 paths C–E). All unsliced.
