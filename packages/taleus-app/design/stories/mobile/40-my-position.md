# User Story: My position

**Stub — not yet written.** See [index.md](index.md).

## Topic
Understanding what I am worth across all tallies, in a unit I choose, and what that number does and
does not mean.

A **single moment**, across everything: assets and liabilities as they stand right now. Distinct from
watching one tally's balance move over a period ([24](24-tally-history.md)) — that is flow through a
relationship; this is a statement of net worth.

## Baseline not to regress
MyCHIPs: the Visual Balance Sheet goal in doc/use-mobile.md — teach net worth and the nature of
value, not just list transactions. That goal is this story's, not 24's: a balance sheet is a
snapshot.

## Open
Cross-unit figures are estimates (domain/rules.md); the story must make that honest without making it useless.
