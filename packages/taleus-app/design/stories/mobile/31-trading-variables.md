# User Story: Trading variables

**Stub — not yet written.** See [index.md](index.md).

## Topic
Setting how much value I want to hold on a tally and what I will pay or accept to move it — the
settings that let my tallies participate in lifts.

These settings *are* the authorization: once signed, lifts happen within them without prompting.
Changing them is an explicit signed act.

## Baseline not to regress
MyCHIPs: TradingVariables (target, bound, reward, clutch) per tally.

## Open
These are the hardest concepts in the system; the story has to make them intuitive without a finance
lecture. A person also has to understand that they are granting standing authority, not just
expressing a preference — and be able to see what that authority currently permits.
