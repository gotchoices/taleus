# User Story: Close a tally

**Stub — not yet written.** See [00-story-map.md](00-story-map.md).

## Topic
Either party ends the trading relationship. With a settled balance it closes; otherwise it stays visible as closing until the balance reaches zero. Close cannot be refused.

## Baseline not to regress
MyCHIPs: closing is requested via a chit, the tally shows as closing (C.open), and closes automatically at zero. A creditor willing to forfeit may send the balance back to close immediately.

## Open
What a party sees while a tally is stuck closing because the other side will not settle.
