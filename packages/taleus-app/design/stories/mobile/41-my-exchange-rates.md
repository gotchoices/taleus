# User Story: My exchange rates

**Stub — not yet written.** See [index.md](index.md).

## Topic
Telling the app how I value one unit against another, for display and for payments that cross units.

Until a party says so, nothing crosses: their tallies in different units settle separately, and no
other party's valuation is applied to their holdings on their behalf. Setting a rate is what opens
that door ([31](31-trading-variables.md) path G).

## Baseline not to regress
No MyCHIPs precedent — MyCHIPs had a single unit of account.

## Open
Whether a user must set rates at all, or whether sensible defaults exist for common currencies.
