#Draft story to give theoretical background for how taleus works


Sam: What's that app you're using?
Jan: Taleus!  It's like Venmo, but without the Venmo
Sam: What do you mean, "without the Venmo"?
Jan: It is just you and the people you transact with, but with no bank in the middle.
Sam: So then who holds the money?
Jan: You, and your partners hold money for each other.
Sam: Like you have to store your friends cash?  Sounds scarey!

Jan: It doesn't work like that.  Most people don't really understand money very well.  Money is just a way of storing value in the present so we can use it later in the future.

There are two kinds of money:
- Things we use like food, materials, energy.  Commodities
- Value promised to us from others, like an IOU.  Credit

Nowadays most all money is credit money, just value owed from one person to another.  This includes pretty much all national currencies like the Dollar and the Euro.

We are used to having banks insulate us from the details so we don't have to think about how it works.  But Taleus allows regular people to interact with each other just like banks deal with other banks--by keeping ledgers of the amounts they owe to each other.

Sam: That sounds amazing, how does it work?
Jan: You can see here, I have $1039 in net value.  But it isn't in a bank account.  It is held by a bunch of my friends and associates.  See this list:  Some people, I owe money to and others owe me.  In my case, more people owe me so I have a net of $1039 I could collect if all the accounts were paid up.

Sam: I can see that.  But how do you get "real money" into the app?
Jan: Just the same way banks do.  You create it?
Sam: You just create it--out of nothing?
Jan: Not out of nothing, out of something real.  Let's assume you want to buy a bike from me.  I'm willing to give you the bike now, but I trust you enough to give you a few weeks to pay me the money.  You give me an IOU for the amount so we can work out actual payment later.

In Taleus, we just record the IOU and that is literally "the money".  That's how it gets "into the app."
Sam: Ahh, so the money didn't come from nothing.  It came from the bike!
Jan: Exactly!

Sam: So what happens when you want me to pay me for real?
Jan: In a sense, you already did by giving me the IOU.  With Taleus, I can use that credit for other things I might want to buy from you.  Let's say you take me out to lunch next week and we want to split the tab.  We just record the IOU now from me to you and it reduces what you owed me for the bike.
Sam: Sounds like a bar tab.
Jan: Now you're getting it!  The really cool thing is that when you get connected to other people besides just me, I can actually "spend" your IOU's not just with you, but with other people you are connected to, either directly or indirectly.  I cand send payments "through you" to other people and this will also reduce the amount you owe me for the bike.

So it really is spendable--real money--as long as you have enough connections I can send it to for people I want to buy things from.

And if you don't, I can always call your tab due if I need you to settle the account.  You can send me Dollars or something else we may find agreeable.

Sam: Sounds great.  How do we get connected?
Jan: Watch (clicks a few prompts on his phone).  Scan this QR code.
ALTERNATE VERSION(s): Jan sends a tally invitation to Sam via text, email, discord, messenger, etc.

Sam scans the code and is navigated to a Sereus.org web site with basic information about Taleus.  He finds the link to the App store and downloads the app.  It launches to show him that he has been "invited to tally" with Jan.  There is an option to proceed or cancel/refuse.

Sam accepts the prompt.  He is asked for some basic information like his name, email address and phone number.  He sees slots for more information like addresses, birthday etc. but they look optional so he proceeds without them.  He completes the form and is back in a home screen where he can see that he has "no money" yet.

Jan has been browsing his social media and sees a notification from Taleus.  He clicks on it and sees that Sam has responded to his tally invitation.
