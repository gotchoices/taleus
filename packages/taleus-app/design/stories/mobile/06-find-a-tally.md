## Topic
Locating one tally among many, when the partner, the unit, or the tally's state is what the user remembers.

## Baseline not to regress
MyCHIPs: TallyReport with Search, plus sort controls (the schema defines a tally-order preference).

## Open
Whether tallies needing attention are separated from the rest, or merely findable among them.
