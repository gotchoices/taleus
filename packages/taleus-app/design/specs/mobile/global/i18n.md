# i18n

## Decisions

- **Library**: i18next + react-i18next; device locale via react-native-localize
- **Locales**: en (default, bundled); further locales bundled as they are contributed
- **Namespaces**: common, screens
- **Key style**: dot-scoped, kebab leaf keys (e.g. `screens.tally-list.empty-message`)
- **RTL**: supported (I18nManager)
- **Locale override**: `?locale=<tag>` on deep links, for demos and scenario capture

## Rules

- No user-visible string is written into a screen. Screens reference keys; specs and stories
  quote English text as intent, not as the string itself.
- Dates, times, and numbers are formatted with `Intl` against the active locale.
- Units of account are **data, not translations** — a unit's name and symbol come from the tally
  (`design/specs/domain/rules.md`) and are never keyed into locale files.
