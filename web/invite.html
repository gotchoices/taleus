What happens next

  1. Install Taleus and enter a name.
  2. Come back to this link (or scan the QR again in the app) to review the offered terms.
  3. Accept, or counter — the tally opens only once both of you have signed.

Invitations are single-use and can be withdrawn, so if this one has already been used you'll need a fresh link from your partner.
